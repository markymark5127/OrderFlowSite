The problem

Still entering customer POs manually?

Most manufacturers still re-key purchase orders from email and PDF into the ERP, then check the same fields a second time.

Retyping customer order information

Customer, PO number, ship-to, and line items get keyed in from a PDF or forwarded email.

Checking pricing by hand

Someone compares the customer PO price to the contract or price list in the ERP.

Matching part numbers

Customer part numbers, descriptions, and revisions have to be reconciled with yours.

Spotting duplicate POs

The same purchase order can arrive twice — and create a second sales order if nobody catches it.

How it works

From customer PO to ERP sales order — with a person on the exceptions.

1

Receive

Customer sends a PO by email or PDF.

2

Extract

OrderFlow reads customer, PO, part, quantity, price, and dates.

3

Validate

Data is checked against ERP records.

4

Review

Exceptions are flagged for human approval.

5

Create

Approved orders are sent to the ERP.
